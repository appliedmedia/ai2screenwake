# AI2ScreenWake artwork

Apple's `iconutil` decoder, not only the ICNS crate, now compares every container representation with its source PNG within two 8-bit premultiplied-RGBA channel levels. The pre-fix 16/32 1x representations failed with a maximum difference of 255 while all eight others matched exactly. Using existing-crate legacy RGB24 plus Mask8 for those two sizes avoids the faulty `icp4`/`icp5` PNG interpretation without a new dependency; all ten corrected representations match exactly. The other eight remain PNG, with twelve exact ICNS chunks for ten images. Genuine decoder/layout reds and corrected pixels remain under `target/polish-fix/`, separate from running-app acceptance.

* [app.svg](<app.svg>): detailed 1024 x 1024 transparent master for the app icon and promotional artwork. U7 places both knobs, speaker slots and LED on the cabinet's left (panel x=163, controls center x=212), with screen bezel x=289 and screen center x=571.5 on the right. Antenna base x=320 rises from the cabinet's left half; both arm vectors, tip circles and angles are preserved. The reflection stays at the screen's top-left and the face is translated, not mirrored. Upper-right Zzz, cabinet, feet, shadow and colors are unchanged. The 1024/16/32/128 exports were rendered and inspected; exact PNG paths are listed in the handling receipt.
* [menu-off.svg](<menu-off.svg>): 24 x 24 transparent monochrome master with closed eyes, a smile and three ascending vector sleep marks at the upper right.
* [menu-on.svg](<menu-on.svg>): matching master with larger wide-open eyeballs, distinct pupils, one straight horizontal mouth line and no sleep marks.
* [menu-error.svg](<menu-error.svg>): matching attention master with X X eyes and a frown, never sleep marks or an exclamation badge.

All three menu faces keep the landscape cabinet and inset screen. Their shared 1.4-stroke antenna rays are shortened 8% around the previous mount, then lifted 0.25 master units to `(6,7.25)`, with tips `(4.16,5.226)` and `(7.472,4.674)`. At the 96-point preview this shortens each ray by approximately one point and lifts the mount one point, not four points. The full round stroke ends just above master y=8, the cabinet top stroke's lower edge, with no antialiased antenna pixels inside the cabinet at any exported density. No horizontal base is added. Cabinet/inset outlines remain one pixel. Cabinet x=0.5/width=23 and screen x=4.5/width=16.5 align their straight edges to 1x columns 0/23 and 4/21. Two filled 1 x 1 selector rectangles at `(2,12)` and `(2,17)` replace blurred circles. On both selector rows the exact 1x alpha map is `255,0,255,0,255` (`#.#.#`); 2x is `255,255,0,0,255,255,0,0,255,255` (`##..##..##`). Menu faces, ears and Off marks are unchanged by U7's separate detailed-app layout. Smallest-z/cabinet clearance remains 1.05 logical pixels, with one/two/four/eight fully transparent rows at 24/48/96/192 pixels.

On keeps 2.4/2.8-radius eyes, 1.1 outlines, distinct 0.85-radius pupils and the single 0.8-stroke mouth from `(9,18.5)` to `(17,18.5)`. Closed/smiling, wide/straight and crossed/frowning faces distinguish Off, On and Error without color. Rust supplies visible `Mode: Can Sleep`, `Mode: No Sleep` or `Off: Error`, retaining face/value through truthful accessible Pending; literal `Off: Error` does not replace independently observed protection truth. The large preview is a real observed-power cycle control; its intentional Error preview requires confirmed Off and never fabricates a fault. Template tinting uses black/alpha negative space, never opaque backgrounds.

`cargo xtask assets` exports these masters as `menu-{off,on,error}{,@2x}.png` at 24/48 pixels and `preview-{off,on,error}{,@2x}.png` at 96/192 pixels under `target/ai2sw/assets`. Every density is independently rasterized from the same state master, not enlarged from a bitmap. Four source masters yield twenty-three encoded outputs, including the refined detailed app icon. `@polish_icon` decodes all ten ICNS images with Apple; `@polish_art` checks controls left of screen and antenna in the left half using absolute source geometry. Their isolated current exports reuse only the existing hash-verified production fixture, without skipping decoder assertions or relaxing the ten-second guard. The `@antenna_fit` requirement checks transformed stroke bounds, isolated round-cap/join antialiasing and decoded exports against the same source without its antenna at all four densities and across all faces. `CompactEarsAndEqualCabinetBorders`, `AwakeEyesAndSingleLineMouth`, and `SmallestSleepMarkClearsCabinet` retain border, mouth, pupil and clear-row proofs. `CrispSelectorPixelsKeepOneColumnGaps` requires exact opaque/transparent left-column alpha on both selector rows at 24/48/96/192 pixels, two disconnected controls, clear interior and legible unchanged faces. `TVScreenLeftEarsSleepRightNoErrorPunctuation` retains Off-only sleep proofs. Inventory/density/black-alpha/provenance/publication checks remain. No fonts, external images or runtime SVG rendering are used; generated assets alone are not physical app acceptance.
