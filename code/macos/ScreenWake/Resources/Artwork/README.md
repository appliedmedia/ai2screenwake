# AI2ScreenWake artwork

* [app.svg](<app.svg>): detailed 1024 x 1024 transparent master for the app icon and promotional artwork. An amber retro cabinet, mint screen, antennas, smile, closed eyes, and rising zzZ establish the identity.
* [menu-off.svg](<menu-off.svg>): 24 x 24 transparent monochrome master with closed eyes, a smile and three ascending vector sleep marks at the upper right.
* [menu-on.svg](<menu-on.svg>): matching master with larger wide-open eyeballs, distinct pupils, one straight horizontal mouth line and no sleep marks.
* [menu-error.svg](<menu-error.svg>): matching attention master with X X eyes and a frown, never sleep marks or an exclamation badge.

All three faces keep the landscape cabinet and inset screen. Their shared 1.4-stroke antenna rays are shortened 8% around the previous mount, then lifted 0.25 master units to `(6,7.25)`, with tips `(4.16,5.226)` and `(7.472,4.674)`. At the 96-point preview this shortens each ray by approximately one point and lifts the mount one point, not four points. The full round stroke ends just above master y=8, the cabinet top stroke's lower edge, with no antialiased antenna pixels inside the cabinet at any exported density. No horizontal base is added. Cabinet/inset outlines remain one pixel. Screen left edge 5.5/right edge 21, both 0.7-radius selector dots and Off sleep marks stay unchanged. Smallest-z/cabinet clearance remains 1.05 logical pixels, with one/two/four/eight fully transparent rows at 24/48/96/192 pixels. The detailed app icon's differently layered antenna is already covered by its filled cabinet and remains unchanged.

On keeps 2.4/2.8-radius eyes, 1.1 outlines, distinct 0.85-radius pupils and the single 0.8-stroke mouth from `(9,18.5)` to `(17,18.5)`. Closed/smiling, wide/straight and crossed/frowning faces distinguish Off, On and Error without color. Rust supplies visible `Mode: Can Sleep`, `Mode: No Sleep` or `Off: Error`, retaining face/value through truthful accessible Pending; literal `Off: Error` does not replace independently observed protection truth. The large preview is a real observed-power cycle control; its intentional Error preview requires confirmed Off and never fabricates a fault. Template tinting uses black/alpha negative space, never opaque backgrounds.

`cargo xtask assets` exports these masters as `menu-{off,on,error}{,@2x}.png` at 24/48 pixels and `preview-{off,on,error}{,@2x}.png` at 96/192 pixels under `target/ai2sw/assets`. Every density is independently rasterized from the same state master, not enlarged from a bitmap. Four source masters yield twenty-three encoded outputs, including the unchanged detailed app icon. The `@antenna_fit` requirement checks actual transformed stroke bounds, isolated round-cap/join antialiasing and decoded exports against the same source without its antenna, at all four densities and across all faces. `CompactEarsAndEqualCabinetBorders`, `AwakeEyesAndSingleLineMouth`, and `SmallestSleepMarkClearsCabinet` pair effective geometry with actual raster silhouette, border ink, continuous mouth/negative space, pupil separation and complete clear-row measurements. `CompactSelectorsRemainTwoIsolatedDots` preserves disconnected controls and their screen gap; `TVScreenLeftEarsSleepRightNoErrorPunctuation` preserves closed-smile/wide-straight/crossed-frown and Off-only sleep proofs. Existing inventory/density/black-alpha/provenance/publication checks remain. White review composites are ignored diagnostics only. No fonts, external images or runtime SVG rendering are used; generated assets alone are not physical app acceptance.
